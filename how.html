How it works

TripForge does not plan your trip. It writes a very good brief, so the AI you already use can plan it with you.

The five steps

  1. Fill in what you know

    Dates, where you are going (or that you do not know yet), how you travel and what you love. Only the first few questions are needed.

  2. Copy the prompt

    Your answers become one prompt, built in your browser. It includes privacy rules for the AI, how to research, and exactly what to build.

  3. Paste it into your AI

    Start a new, temporary chat in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another AI, and paste. Switch on web search if your AI has it.

  4. Your AI researches and asks

    It looks up things to do, runs, hikes, webcams and events, proposes an outline, and asks you a batch of questions. Nothing is built until you say OK.

  5. It builds your planner

    In the format you chose, with placeholders like [[ADD LOCALLY: hotel booking reference]] for the private bits you fill in yourself.

Why your own AI?

Your AI can search the web as it is today, talk it through with you, and change the plan when you change your mind. TripForge just makes sure it starts with a good brief and safe habits.

Weather, webcams and routes

  • Weather comes from Yr.no (MET Norway) and Open-Meteo. Both are free. A hosted site built by a coding agent can fetch Yr data on a schedule; other formats show Open-Meteo live and link to Yr.
  • Webcams are only added when your AI finds a real one. No webcam, no section.
  • Runs and hikes link to published routes. Only a coding agent building a website draws its own routes, with a proper routing engine. Chat AIs are told never to invent GPS coordinates.
