What your AI can build for you

Day-by-day plans

Morning, afternoon and evening for every day, with a plan B for bad weather.

Weather & webcams

Forecasts from Yr.no and Open-Meteo, typical climate for later dates, and live webcams where they exist.

Runs & hikes

Morning runs from where you stay, and hikes with walking times and a bad-weather backup.

Checklists

Packing lists for each traveller, plus a pre-trip checklist that counts down to departure.

Private by design

TripForge never asks for booking references, flight numbers or names, and the prompt tells your AI not to ask either. Your planner can be a file on your phone, a page in your chat, a printout, or a website behind an email login.
