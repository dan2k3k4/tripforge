What your AI can build for you

  • Day-by-day plans with a plan B for rain.
  • Weather and webcams from Yr.no and Open-Meteo.
  • Runs and hikes from where you sleep.
  • Packing lists for each traveller.
  • Local tips and phrases, holidays and events.
  • Private by default: a file, a chat page, a printout, or a site behind a login.

Share the trip, not your passport

TripForge never asks for booking references, flight numbers or names, and the prompt tells your AI not to ask either. It leaves placeholders you fill in on your own device.
